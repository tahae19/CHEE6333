1. Learning Objective & Scope

Learning objective: calculate velocity fields for one-dimensional flows of non-Newtonian fluids.

Source map: Handout 07, p. 1; lecture transcript opening.

The lecture extends the momentum-transport machinery from earlier handouts. The main change is constitutive: viscosity is no longer assumed to be a constant material parameter. Instead, the apparent viscosity may depend on the deformation rate.

Important

For the non-Newtonian examples in this handout, start from the general momentum equation in terms of the stress tensor, not the constant-viscosity Navier–Stokes form.

فکر اصلی این جلسه این است که روش حل قبلی عوض نشده است؛ چیزی که عوض می‌شود رابطهٔ میان تنش و نرخ تغییر شکل است. بنابراین ترتیب حذف جمله‌ها و اعمال شرایط مرزی همچنان اهمیت اصلی را دارد.

2. From Newtonian to Non-Newtonian Fluids

2.1 Recall: Newton’s law of viscosity

Newtonian fluids are characterized by viscosity and dilatational viscosity . The lecture notes that the dilatational term is mostly ignored for liquids or ideal gases.

2.2 Why non-Newtonian?

Biological fluids

Blood and cellular biopolymers such as actin.

Suspensions and process fluids

Cement, concrete, drilling fluids, fracturing fluids, and asphaltenes.

Consumer materials

Food and personal-care products.

The lecture movies emphasize deviations from Newtonian behavior: oobleck is used as a shear-thickening example; polymer solutions show the Weissenberg, Barus, and Kaye effects. The lecture attributes the polymer examples to nonzero first normal stresses.

یک سیال غیرنیوتنی لزوماً فقط «گرانروی متفاوت» ندارد. نکتهٔ اصلی این است که پاسخ مکانیکی آن می‌تواند به نرخ تغییر شکل وابسته باشد و در بعضی مواد رفتارهایی دیده می‌شود که با یک گرانروی ثابت قابل توصیف نیست.

3. Generalized Newtonian Fluid Model

Schematic rheometer interpretation: impose deformation rate, infer apparent viscosity.

The generalized Newtonian model preserves the Newtonian tensorial form but replaces the constant viscosity with an apparent viscosity . The scalar is obtained from the tensor double-dot product.

Remember

The tensor component can be signed. The scalar magnitude defined by the square root is nonnegative.

برای حل مسئله ابتدا مؤلفه‌های غیرصفر تانسور نرخ کرنش را از میدان سرعت پیدا کن. سپس اندازهٔ اسکالر را بساز. بعد از آن گرانروی ظاهری از مدل تجربی به دست می‌آید و در رابطهٔ تنش جایگذاری می‌شود.

3

4

Exam

The recurring exam skill is not memorizing a finished velocity profile. It is reproducing the sequence: assumptions → nonzero component → governing equation → constitutive law → BCs → result.

13. What I Need to Know for the Exam

Must be able to reproduce
  • Write the generalized Newtonian constitutive model and define both and .
  • Recognize when the general stress-tensor form of the momentum equation is required.
  • For a 1-D flow, identify the one relevant stress component from the momentum direction and the direction of spatial variation.
  • Compute the sign of the scalar shear-rate expression from the geometry rather than guessing it.
  • Derive the power-law tube velocity profile from the stress distribution and no-slip BC.
  • Set up mass-flow and torque integrals with the correct area element and lever arm.
  • Apply both no-slip BCs in Couette flow.
  • Map cone-and-plate flow to parallel plates under the small-angle approximation.
  • Show why cone-and-plate flow has one unique nonzero rate-of-strain component and uniform shear rate.
  • Check every power-law result with and .
Do not memorize blindly

The lecture repeatedly builds solutions by eliminating terms from the governing equations. A memorized final formula is much less reliable than knowing why each term is zero and where each BC is applied.

برای امتحان بیشترین ارزش را به مسیر حل بده. باید بتوانی از روی شکل، مؤلفهٔ سرعت، جهت تغییر، مؤلفهٔ تنش و شرط‌های مرزی را تشخیص بدهی. فرمول نهایی باید نتیجهٔ این تشخیص‌ها باشد، نه نقطهٔ شروع.
