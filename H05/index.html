1 The Continuity Equation

English

From a microscopic mass balance:

\[ \frac{\partial\rho}{\partial t} = -\nabla\cdot(\rho\mathbf{v}) \]
(1)

Term 1: rate of increase of mass per unit volume. Term 2: net rate of mass addition per unit volume by convection. For an incompressible fluid (\(d\rho/dt=0\), \(\rho\) constant, brought outside the divergence):

\[ \boxed{\ \nabla\cdot\mathbf{v}=0\ } \]
(2)
توضیح فارسی
معادله‌ی پیوستگی از بالانس جرم میکروسکوپیک به‌دست می‌آید: جمله‌ی اول نرخ افزایش جرم در واحد حجم، جمله‌ی دوم نرخ خالص اضافه‌شدن جرم به‌واسطه‌ی جابه‌جایی است. برای سیال تراکم‌ناپذیر (چگالی ثابت)، این معادله به سادگی \(\nabla\cdot\mathbf{v}=0\) تبدیل می‌شود.

2 The Equation of Motion (Cauchy's Equation)

English

From a microscopic momentum balance:

\[ \frac{\partial}{\partial t}(\rho\mathbf{v}) = -\nabla\cdot(\rho\mathbf{v}\mathbf{v}) - \nabla p - \nabla\cdot\boldsymbol\tau + \rho\mathbf{g} \]
(3)

Term-by-term physical meaning: (1) rate of increase of momentum per unit volume; (2) rate of momentum addition per unit volume by convection (note \(\rho vv\) is exactly the convective term of the combined momentum flux tensor); (3)–(4) rate of momentum addition per unit volume by molecular transport (pressure and viscous stress); (5) external force per unit volume from gravity.

توضیح فارسی
معادله‌ی حرکت از بالانس مومنتوم میکروسکوپیک به‌دست می‌آید. جمله‌ی اول نرخ افزایش مومنتوم؛ جمله‌ی دوم افزودن مومنتوم به‌واسطه‌ی جابه‌جایی (که دقیقاً همان جمله‌ی جابه‌جایی در تانسور فلاکس مومنتوم ترکیبی است)؛ جملات سوم و چهارم انتقال مولکولی (فشار و تنش ویسکوز)؛ و جمله‌ی آخر نیروی خارجی گرانش.

3 The Substantial Derivative

English

Defined as an operator:

\[ \frac{D}{Dt} \equiv \frac{\partial}{\partial t} + \mathbf{v}\cdot\nabla \]
(4)

The first term is the local acceleration; the second is the convective acceleration. For a scalar field \(c(x,y,z,t)\), applying the chain rule to \(dc/dt\) along the path of an observer moving with the fluid (\(dx/dt=v_x\), etc.) recovers exactly this combination — the substantial derivative is the rate of change of \(c\) as seen by an observer moving with the fluid.

Using the continuity equation, one can show for any scalar \(f\):

\[ \frac{\partial(\rho f)}{\partial t} + \nabla\cdot(\rho\mathbf{v}f) = \rho\frac{Df}{Dt} \]
(5)

(the same holds component-wise for a vector \(\mathbf{f}\)). This lets the continuity and motion equations be rewritten compactly:

\[ \frac{D\rho}{Dt} = -\rho\nabla\cdot\mathbf{v}, \qquad \rho\frac{D\mathbf{v}}{Dt} = -\nabla p - \nabla\cdot\boldsymbol\tau + \rho\mathbf{g} \]
(6)
Remember

These are the forms most commonly seen in textbooks. Recognize that Eq. (3) and the second equation of (6) are the same physical statement, just rearranged using the product rule and the continuity equation.

توضیح فارسی
مشتق ماده‌ای، تغییرات یک کمیت را از دید ناظری که همراه سیال حرکت می‌کند نشان می‌دهد؛ شامل یک جزء موضعی (تغییر با زمان در یک نقطه‌ی ثابت) و یک جزء جابه‌جایی (\(\mathbf{v}\cdot\nabla\)) است. با استفاده از این تعریف و معادله‌ی پیوستگی، معادلات پیوستگی و حرکت به فرم فشرده‌تری بازنویسی می‌شوند که در بیشتر کتاب‌های درسی دیده می‌شود.

4 Navier–Stokes and Its Simplified Limits

English

4.1 — Navier–Stokes (constant \(\rho,\mu\))

\[ \rho\frac{D\mathbf{v}}{Dt} = -\nabla\mathscr{P} + \mu\nabla^2\mathbf{v} \]
(7)

using the combined pressure \(\mathscr{P}\) to fold gravity into the pressure gradient term.

4.2 — Creeping flow (Stokes equation): neglect acceleration

\[ 0 = -\nabla p + \mu\nabla^2\mathbf{v} + \rho\mathbf{g} \]
(8)

Used in lubrication theory, particle motion in suspensions, flow in porous media, and microorganism swimming (the physical origin of the name "creeping flow" becomes clear once dimensionless numbers are introduced in Handout 6).

4.3 — Euler equation: neglect viscous term (\(\nabla\cdot\boldsymbol\tau=0\), inviscid fluid)

\[ \rho\frac{D\mathbf{v}}{Dt} = -\nabla p + \rho\mathbf{g} \]
(9)

Used for flow around airfoils (away from the surface), flow around bridge abutments, compressible gas dynamics, and ocean currents.

توضیح فارسی
معادله‌ی ناویه–استوکس برای چگالی و ویسکوزیته‌ی ثابت نوشته می‌شود. با حذف جمله‌ی شتاب (سمت چپ)، معادله‌ی «جریان خزنده» یا استوکس به‌دست می‌آید که در تئوری روان‌کاری، حرکت ذرات در سوسپانسیون، جریان در محیط متخلخل، و شنای میکروارگانیسم‌ها کاربرد دارد. با حذف جمله‌ی ویسکوز (سیال غیرلزج)، معادله‌ی اویلر به‌دست می‌آید که برای جریان اطراف بال هواپیما، جریان رودخانه اطراف پایه‌ی پل، دینامیک گاز تراکم‌پذیر، و جریان‌های اقیانوسی به کار می‌رود.

5 Revisiting Pipe Flow via Navier–Stokes

English

Postulate: flow only in \(z\), radially symmetric (no \(\theta\)-dependence), no radial/tangential flow. Continuity in cylindrical coordinates (with \(v_r=v_\theta=0\), \(\rho\) constant) gives \(\partial v_z/\partial z=0\): \(v_z\) depends only on \(r\).

Crossing out terms in the \(r\)- and \(\theta\)-equations of motion (using \(v_r=v_\theta=0\)) leaves \(\partial\mathscr{P}/\partial r=0\) and \(\partial\mathscr{P}/\partial\theta=0\): the combined pressure depends only on \(z\). The \(z\)-equation of motion becomes:

\[ 0 = -\frac{d\mathscr{P}}{dz} + \mu\left(\frac1r\frac{d}{dr}\Big(r\frac{dv_z}{dr}\Big)\right) \]
(10)

The left piece is a function of \(z\) only; the right piece is a function of \(r\) only. A function of \(z\) can equal a function of \(r\) only if both equal the same constant. Integrating both resulting equations:

\[ \mathscr{P}(z) = C_0 z + C_1, \qquad v_z(r) = \frac{C_0}{4\mu}r^2 + C_2\ln r + C_3 \]
(11)

Four unknowns require four boundary conditions: \(\mathscr{P}(0)=\mathscr{P}_0\), \(\mathscr{P}(L)=\mathscr{P}_L\) (fixing \(C_0,C_1\)); \(v_z(R)=0\) (no-slip); and finiteness of \(v_z\) at \(r=0\) (forcing \(C_2=0\), since \(\ln r\) diverges there). The result:

\[ \boxed{\ v_z(r) = \frac{\mathscr{P}_0-\mathscr{P}_L}{4\mu L}R^2\left[1-\left(\frac{r}{R}\right)^2\right]\ } \]
(12)

— exactly the Handout 4 shell-balance result, obtained here more directly from the general equations of motion. Validity: laminar flow (\(Re \lesssim 2300\)), far from entrance/exit — the same conditions imposed for the shell-balance derivation.

Key Insight

Navier–Stokes reaches the same answer as the shell balance faster because it starts from the general equations rather than re-deriving a balance from scratch — but it requires already knowing the vector-calculus forms of the operators in the relevant coordinate system (see BSL/Deen appendices).

توضیح فارسی
با فرض تقارن استوانه‌ای و عدم وابستگی به \(\theta\)، معادله‌ی پیوستگی نشان می‌دهد \(v_z\) فقط تابع \(r\) است. معادلات حرکت \(r\) و \(\theta\) نشان می‌دهند فشار ترکیبی فقط تابع \(z\) است. معادله‌ی حرکت \(z\) به‌صورت «تابعی از \(z\) برابر تابعی از \(r\)» درمی‌آید که فقط وقتی هر دو برابر یک ثابت باشند برقرار است. با چهار شرط مرزی (دو مربوط به فشار، عدم‌لغزش، و تناهی در مرکز)، همان نتیجه‌ی هاگن–پواسویِ به‌دست‌آمده از شل بالانس دوباره حاصل می‌شود — این‌بار مستقیماً از معادلات کلی حرکت.

6 The Fanning Friction Factor

English

Defined as a dimensionless ratio of shear stress to kinetic energy density:

\[ f \equiv \frac{|\text{shear stress}|}{\rho\langle v_z\rangle^2/2} = \left|\frac{d\mathscr{P}}{dz}\right|\frac{R}{L}\cdot\frac{1}{\rho\langle v_z\rangle^2} \]
(13)

Substituting the pipe-flow average velocity \(\langle v_z\rangle=(\mathscr{P}_0-\mathscr{P}_L)R^2/8\mu L\) to eliminate the pressure drop, then substituting the pipe Reynolds number \(Re=2R\langle v_z\rangle\rho/\mu\):

\[ \boxed{\ f = \frac{16}{Re}\ } \qquad \text{(laminar pipe flow)} \]
(14)
توضیح فارسی
ضریب اصطکاک فنینگ نسبت بی‌بعد تنش برشی به چگالی انرژی جنبشی است. با جایگذاری سرعت متوسط جریان لوله و تعریف عدد رینولدز، رابطه‌ی معروف \(f=16/Re\) برای جریان آرام در لوله به‌دست می‌آید.

7 New Geometry: the Couette Viscometer

English

Motivation: measuring the (possibly shear-rate-dependent) viscosity of complex fluids (suspensions, polymer solutions/melts) — the field of rheology. Geometry: outer cylinder (radius \(R\)) rotates at angular velocity \(\Omega_0\); inner cylinder (radius \(\kappa R\), \(0<\kappa<1\)) is stationary.

Assumptions: \(v_\theta=v_\theta(r)\) only, \(v_r=v_z=0\), cylindrically symmetric. The \(\theta\)-equation of motion reduces to:

\[ 0 = \frac{d}{dr}\left(\frac1r\frac{d}{dr}(rv_\theta)\right) \]
(15)

"Peeling off" derivatives twice gives \(v_\theta(r) = \tfrac12 C_1 r + C_2/r\). Applying no-slip at both walls (\(v_\theta(\kappa R)=0\), \(v_\theta(R)=\Omega_0 R\)):

\[ \boxed{\ v_\theta(r) = \Omega_0 R\left[\frac{r/(\kappa R)-\kappa R/r}{1/\kappa-\kappa}\right]\ } \]
(16)

The relevant viscous stress is \(\tau_{r\theta} = -\mu r\,\dfrac{d}{dr}\!\left(\dfrac{v_\theta}{r}\right)\), evaluated to give:

\[ \tau_{r\theta} = -2\mu\Omega_0\left(\frac{R}{r}\right)^2\frac{\kappa^2}{1-\kappa^2} \]
(17)

Torque on the inner cylinder (force = stress × area, torque = force × lever arm \(\kappa R\), with a minus sign by the sign convention since the fluid at greater \(r\) acts on the cylinder at lesser \(r\)):

\[ \boxed{\ T = 4\pi\mu\Omega_0 R^2 L\,\frac{\kappa^2}{1-\kappa^2}\ } \]
(18)
Exam Note / Challenge Problem

The lecture also poses (as a challenge, not worked in the recording) the opposite configuration — inner cylinder rotating at \(\Omega_i\), outer cylinder fixed — giving \(v_\theta(r)=\Omega_i\kappa R\big[(R/r)-(r/R)\big]/(1/\kappa-\kappa)\); and a narrow-gap limit (\(\kappa=1-\epsilon\), \(\epsilon\ll1\)) that should recover planar Couette flow.

توضیح فارسی
ویسکومتر کوئت برای اندازه‌گیری ویسکوزیته‌ی سیالات پیچیده (رئولوژی) استفاده می‌شود. استوانه‌ی بیرونی با سرعت زاویه‌ای \(\Omega_0\) می‌چرخد و استوانه‌ی داخلی ثابت است. با حل معادله‌ی حرکت \(\theta\)، پروفایل سرعت مماسی و سپس تنش برشی و گشتاور روی استوانه‌ی داخلی به‌دست می‌آید. حالت معکوس (چرخش استوانه‌ی داخلی) و حد شکاف باریک به‌عنوان تمرین چالشی مطرح شده‌اند.

8 Key Equations

Continuity (incompressible)

\[ \nabla\cdot\mathbf{v}=0 \]

Navier–Stokes

\[ \rho\frac{D\mathbf{v}}{Dt} = -\nabla\mathscr{P} + \mu\nabla^2\mathbf{v} \]

Creeping flow / Euler limits

\[ 0=-\nabla p+\mu\nabla^2\mathbf{v}+\rho\mathbf{g} \qquad\text{(creeping)}, \qquad \rho\frac{D\mathbf{v}}{Dt}=-\nabla p+\rho\mathbf{g} \qquad\text{(Euler)} \]

Fanning friction factor (laminar pipe flow)

\[ f = 16/Re \]

Couette viscometer torque

\[ T = 4\pi\mu\Omega_0 R^2 L\,\frac{\kappa^2}{1-\kappa^2} \]

9 What I Need to Know for the Exam

English
  • Be able to state the continuity and Navier–Stokes equations, and identify which term represents which physical mechanism.
  • Know the definition of the substantial derivative and be able to derive it from first principles (chain rule along a fluid path).
  • Be able to identify which simplified version of Navier–Stokes (full, creeping flow, Euler) applies to a given physical situation, and name at least one application of each.
  • Be able to re-derive the Hagen–Poiseuille pipe-flow profile starting from the general equations of motion in cylindrical coordinates (not the shell balance) — including the "function of \(z\) = function of \(r\) = constant" argument and all four boundary conditions.
  • Know the Fanning friction factor definition and be able to derive \(f=16/Re\) for laminar pipe flow.
  • Be able to set up and solve the Couette viscometer problem: postulate the velocity field, reduce Navier–Stokes, apply no-slip at both cylinders, and compute the torque on the inner cylinder including the correct sign convention.
